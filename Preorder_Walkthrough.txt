Pre-order Metafields Walkthrough

This guide explains how we created and utilized custom Shopify metafields to completely automate the pre-order logic for your store. 

1. Creating the Metafields
We defined four new Product Metafields in your Shopify admin settings to act as our "schedule" for pre-orders. 

The metafields we used are:
* Preorder Start Time (custom.preorder_start_time): When the pre-order officially opens (Date and time).
* Preorder End Time (custom.preorder_end_time): When the pre-order window closes (Date and time).
* General Sales Time (custom.preorder_general_sales_time): When the product becomes a regular item for general sales (Date and time).
* Preorder Dispatch Date (custom.preorder_dispatch_date): The estimated shipping or dispatch date for pre-order fulfillment (Single line text or Date).

To recreate or edit these definitions:
1. Go to your Shopify Admin.
2. Navigate to Settings > Custom data.
3. Click on Products.
4. Add the definitions mentioned above.

2. Setting the Schedules on Products
Once the metafield definitions exist, every product in your store gains these fields at the very bottom of its editing page in the Shopify admin.

To schedule a pre-order:
1. Open the product you want to put on pre-order.
2. Scroll down to the Metafields section.
3. Set the exact Start Time, End Time, General Sales Time, and the expected Dispatch Date.
4. Save the product.

3. How the Theme Utilizes the Metafields
We injected a custom Liquid script across all critical areas of your theme (Product Page, Product Cards, Cart, and Inventory logic) to read these metafield dates and compare them to the current time.

The theme instantly calculates which Preorder State the product is currently in. Furthermore, it automatically reads the Preorder Dispatch Date and injects it into a descriptive message directly underneath the Add to Cart button (e.g., "Shipping starts July 31, 2026—general sales open...").

The states behave as follows:

State 1: Upcoming (Not Yet Open)
* Condition: Current time is before the start_time.
* Behavior: 
  - The Add to Cart button disables itself and reads PRE ORDERS OPEN [DATE] AT [TIME].
  - The Dispatch Date message is displayed under the button.
  - The Quantity Picker completely hides itself.
  - The Inventory Progress Bar greys out with no text.
  - The Quick Add button on collection pages is replaced with a button that says Pre-order soon and links to the product page.
  - If a customer somehow has this item in their cart (e.g., from an old session), a red warning appears and checkout is blocked.

State 2: Open (Ongoing)
* Condition: Current time is between the start_time and end_time.
* Behavior:
  - The Add to Cart button activates and behaves normally.
  - The Dispatch Date message is displayed under the button.
  - The Quantity Picker is visible.
  - The Inventory Progress Bar turns orange (warning color) and reads Preorder ongoing.
  - The Quick Add button on collection pages is replaced with a button that says Pre-order ongoing and links to the product page (forcing the customer to view dispatch details before buying).
  - The cart allows checkout.

State 3: Closed
* Condition: Current time is after the end_time but before the general_sales_time.
* Behavior:
  - The Add to Cart button disables itself and reads PRE ORDERS CLOSED.
  - The Dispatch Date message is displayed under the button.
  - The Quantity Picker completely hides itself.
  - The Inventory Progress Bar greys out with no text.
  - The Quick Add button on collection pages is replaced with a button that says Pre-order closed and links to the product page.
  - Checkout is strictly blocked if the item is in the cart.

State 0: General Sales (Standard Behavior)
* Condition: Current time is after the general_sales_time (or general_sales_time is empty but end_time has passed).
* Behavior:
  - All pre-order overrides are completely disabled.
  - The Dispatch Date message is removed.
  - The product returns to its standard Shopify behavior, showing normal "In Stock" or "Sold Out" text based on your actual inventory levels.
  - Quick Add grid buttons reappear, allowing standard fast-checkout.
